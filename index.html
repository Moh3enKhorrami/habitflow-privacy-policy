Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026
Developer: Mohsen Khorrami

Your habit records are stored locally on your device. HabitFlow does not require an account. Remote notifications use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service, which process technical information needed to deliver notifications.

1. Information stored on your device

HabitFlow stores habits, completion and streak history, reminders, notes, mood entries, routines, challenges, badges, and app preferences in local app storage. We do not upload your habit database to a developer-operated server or to Firebase for notification delivery.

2. Notifications and Firebase

With your permission, HabitFlow displays local habit reminders and can receive remote push notifications. Local reminders are scheduled on your device.

Remote notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs). These services process technical information such as Firebase installation identifiers, notification registration tokens, IP addresses, and service connection information to register the app installation and deliver messages. HabitFlow subscribes installations to a notification topic based on the app language. Your habit names, notes, mood entries, and health records are not included in that subscription.

You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling alerts does not necessarily stop all technical communication with the messaging service while the app is installed.

Learn more in Firebase Privacy and Security and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

3. Optional device permissions

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice-based habit logging, HabitFlow requests microphone and speech recognition access. Recognition is provided by the operating system and may use its provider’s servers depending on device capabilities and settings. HabitFlow does not save audio recordings in its habit database or send them to a developer-operated server. Recognized text is used to match and complete a habit.

Calendar

If you enable calendar integration, HabitFlow requests calendar access to create habit events. Events may sync through your selected calendar account according to that account’s settings. HabitFlow does not upload your calendar to a developer-operated server.

Health

Health integration is currently disabled in this release. HabitFlow does not currently read HealthKit data. If this changes in a future release, this policy will be updated and the app will request the required permission.

You can manage device permissions in iOS Settings.

4. Backups and exports

You can export habit data and choose where to save or share it. Exported files may contain personal habit information. Any service you choose to receive a file handles it under its own policy.

App data and locally created backup files may be included in device or iCloud backups if enabled in your Apple settings. This depends on your device configuration; HabitFlow does not provide a separate developer-hosted cloud database or cross-device synchronization service.

5. Analytics, advertising, and accounts

HabitFlow does not include advertising, advertising tracking, Firebase Analytics, or Crashlytics. Firebase Cloud Messaging is used for notification delivery. HabitFlow does not require user registration or login.

6. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Support email is handled through Gmail under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Local habit information remains on your device until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Offloading the app may preserve its data. Backups and exported copies must be managed separately in the services or locations where you saved them. Notification service information is retained according to the relevant provider’s policies.

8. Children’s privacy

HabitFlow is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can investigate and remove that information where appropriate.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
